Smash Bender changelog

2026-10-29, v2026.10.29. Word timing now uses the real lyrics and a word map, so the second song is cut on real word edges and sung on the first song's timing and notes. Songs can be uploaded from the phone, a short job starter and a downloader were added, and a scorer matches words by content.

2026-10-27, v2026.10.27. First release. A server that splits a song, reads its melody, bends a vocal, sings it, and mixes the result. Songs can be fetched from direct links, clips are 30 seconds, and the loudness and vocal balance are even.
